How to read (mind the difference from the probe cell above):
  · In the probe, "no form" only strips the decision signal and keeps the form line in the
    embedding text, measuring method five at 57.5%.
    The real switch wipes form out of the embedding text too; method five then measures 55.4%.
    Conclusion: that probe cell cannot stand in for --no-form -- the two differ by 2.1pp.
  · Four of the five methods actually gain completeness with form off. form is a hard veto: 45
    rows whose form is judged UNKNOWN, plus the misjudgements caused by SEO keyword stuffing,
    keep genuinely identical products apart; drop it and those pairs merge.
  · ⚠️ But method_3_semantic's adversarial regression drops from 7/7 to 4/7, which by itself
    jams the release gate. What form was holding back is exactly those 3 hand-verified real errors.
  · Only method_5_hybrid gets worse with form off (-6.3pp). It late-fuses three signal channels,
    and there form's hard constraint is a net gain.

What this actually means for "scaling to the few hundred categories of the whole site":
  form being unmaintainable ≠ dropping it is harmless. Dropping it lifts completeness on average,
  but it opens up the defence line on the adversarial cases. The right path is still to replace the
  hand-written dictionary with a learned classifier, not to simply switch it off.
